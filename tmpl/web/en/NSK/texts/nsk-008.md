# Sources and limits of use

Three external pages were checked on 15 September 2026. They help explain terms and an accounting analogy. None confirms NSK's effectiveness.

| Source | What is used | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [Talgat: full NSK article, corpus 0.1.5 dated 22 September 2026](/NSK/en/texts/nsk-003/) | The author's concept, formulas and 25 answers | The current author text is approved for publication; empirical effects require separate testing |
| [Author's LiveJournal archive: talgaton](https://talgaton.livejournal.com/) | Historical source of the author's texts, including early NSK formulations | The archive does not define the current revision; that is defined by the published author corpus |
| [BIEN: About basic income](https://basicincome.org/about-basic-income/) | Definition of basic income as an individual universal payment without a means test or work requirement | It defines a different category of mechanisms and does not endorse NSK |
| [OECD: Financial disincentive to return to work](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/financial-disincentive-to-return-to-work.html) | Loss of some earnings when benefits decrease and taxes rise on returning to work | The indicator uses a specific family scenario; its numerical values are not used in the NSK article |
| [Federal Tax Service: Value Added Tax](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/taxation/taxes/nds/) | Tax credit and reimbursement as an accounting analogy | NSK is not the current VAT procedure; FTS rates, deadlines and examples are not applied to NSK |

The concept is connected with the author's 2010 text and 2026 clarifications. Historical files and original messages remain in the local editorial archive. The author's report describes how these items were selected and where they diverge. This description does not authorise exporting the archive.

A local overview of social trends and AI materials are retained for future research. The overview's empirical data are not part of this first concise corpus. A separate publication would require checking sources and dates and assessing the range in which the data apply to NSK.

The author chooses the terms for using their texts. External sources are linked and briefly described. Their pages, illustrations and archive comments are not copied.
