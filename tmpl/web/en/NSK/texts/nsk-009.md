# About the author and preparation of the materials

The New Social Contract concept was created by **Talgat Robertovich Zainiev, Candidate of Economic Sciences**.

The author developed the NSK concept in 1998. Its first publication appeared in 2010; the current working revision was prepared in 2026. GPT-6 in Codex helped edit materials, prepare explanations and catalogue entries, and check consistency. AI is not an independent expert and does not replace the author's approval.

The author can be contacted about the project via [GitHub](https://github.com/talgatiko), [email](mailto:zajniev.talgat@gmail.com), or [Telegram @talgatiko](https://t.me/talgatiko).
