# NSK concept passport

Material security for everyone and earnings on top of it. The concept was created by Talgat. This publication is based on revision v2 and later limits adopted for the approved outreach plan.

## Meaning

| Proposition | Status | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| The same base belongs to every person from birth, regardless of income | Author's principle | Entitlement does not depend on a means test, usefulness or productivity |
| The employer transfers the full base and receives an equal credit, with reimbursement | Proposed mechanism rule | Under the current rules, payment cannot be capped at the firm's tax liability |
| `Y = B + a` | Accounting identity under the stated rules | Full payment of the base does not determine the supplement or how prior wages change |
| One person receives one base per period; a child's base is paid through a representative | Author's principle and accounting rule | Multiple jobs and transfers between payers do not create a second base |
| Charitable employers may become an additional channel | Author's assumption | NSK provides the person's base; personal initiative may organise support and charity may cover administration |
| More hiring options, mobility and changes in social relations | Expected effects proposed by the author | Their size and persistence require research into a specific implementation |
| `B = 42` when the average wage is 100 | Illustrative assumption | This is not current Russian statistics, a cash proposal or a condition of entitlement |
| Base size, financing, and interaction with pensions and benefits | Open legislative choices | The corpus does not prescribe cancelling payments or a particular tax |

## Limits of inference

Cash-flow arithmetic must be checked separately from the behaviour of people and businesses. Research on basic income, AI and work provides context but does not test NSK. Public support, mechanism effectiveness and readiness for implementation require separate evidence. Supporting life has an independent rationale; NSK should not be assessed only by productive employment.

## Terms used in this corpus

**Base:** the same personal amount. **Supplement:** earnings above the base. **Credit:** reduction of the employer's accrued tax liability by the amount of bases paid. **Reimbursement:** payment of the amount by which the credit exceeds accrued taxes. **Payer:** the organisation that directly administers the base. This payment role does not make the organisation the owner of the personal right.

A public glossary and status page remain proposals from section 15. These definitions are included here to check consistency across the required corpus.
